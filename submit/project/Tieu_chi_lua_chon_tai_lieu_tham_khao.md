# Literature Selection Criteria

**Study:** Q10 - AI-Assisted Cache Mapping for Continuous Data Streams.  
**Search interval:** 1 January 2023 through 31 October 2026.  
**Selected corpus:** nine distinct publications. **Submission edition:** 1 November 2026.

These criteria are the group's selection rules rather than an additional claim about instructor requirements. The nine-paper corpus supersedes the preliminary numerical target of six to eight papers. The original criteria remain preserved in the historical evidence archive.

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Operational condition |
|---|---|
| Publication date | The cited journal or proceedings edition falls in the declared interval. Acceptance dates, DOI substrings, upload dates, and search-engine timestamps do not determine eligibility. |
| Document type | Peer-reviewed research article or scholarly review in a named journal or conference proceedings. |
| Publication identity | Authors, title, venue, year, and DOI or stable publisher record are identifiable. |
| Relevance | The work directly informs cache mapping/indexing, associativity, conflict behavior, access-stream evaluation, or AI/ML-supported cache management. |
| Methodological substance | Architecture or algorithm, evaluation setup, comparison baseline, and outcome measures are available for appraisal. |
| Accessible full text | A lawful publisher, institutional, or author version permits examination beyond the abstract. |
| Attribution | Findings retain their original cache type, workload, baseline, and experimental context. |
| Publication status | Available publisher notices and metadata are checked for corrections or retractions. An unavailable page or missing notice is not treated as a guarantee of status. |
| Distinctness | Multiple versions of substantially the same study are not counted as independent evidence. |

IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, established specialist journals, and conference proceedings are preferred publication sources. Google Scholar is a discovery service, not a publisher or a quality guarantee. Institutional affiliation is recorded where available, but prestige alone is neither sufficient nor necessary for inclusion. No minimum citation count is imposed.

## Review procedure

The targeted search identifies candidates by title, abstract, and keywords, followed by verification of publication identity and inspection of full text. The appraisal records the problem, contribution, evaluation context, limitations, and relation to Q10. Related instruction caches, micro-operation caches, security caches, and software item caches are explicitly labeled. Their published performance numbers are not substituted for results of the project's data-cache model.

The review is purposive rather than systematic. It does not claim complete coverage of 2023-2026 research. Publication status was assessed using accessible records and saved source material; access limitations remain documented in submission QA.

## Corpus composition and responsibilities

| Theme | Studies | Assigned responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Placement, conflicts, and resource accounting | LIT-001 to LIT-003 | Trương Tô Dân, QE210127 |
| Application context, evaluation, and miss consequences | LIT-004 to LIT-006 | Trần Châu Hoàng Nam, QE210145 |
| Machine learning and management overhead | LIT-007 to LIT-009 | Dương Quốc Thịnh, QE210121 |

The assignment is not evidence of individual manual completion. AI-assisted preparation is disclosed. Each detailed appraisal contains at least 800 prose words, its own named conclusion, and publication links. An overall conclusion synthesizes the nine studies. The English report contains a separate, shorter Literature Review that follows the supplied report-length requirements.

Preprints without peer review, blogs, discussion posts, promotional descriptions, and AI responses are not primary scholarly evidence. Older sources may support background only if explicitly identified as exceptions; none is counted among these nine main studies. The AI records document the design process rather than functioning as research-paper citations.
